About Us

We're Manufacturing All Types of Industrial Springs

Your Trusted and Reliable Springs Partner

Mody Spring Works is one of Hyderabad's leading manufacturers of industrial springs. With skilled craftsmen, carefully selected raw materials, and ISO 9001 quality systems, we produce compression, tension, conical, torsion, barrel, and touch-sensor springs, along with wireforms, R-pins, switch springs, and air compressor valve plates.

Every product is inspected and tested before dispatch. We supply across India and support export requirements with flexible manufacturing for OEM and custom specifications.

Our primary objective is client happiness. We strive to meet the highest standards set by clients throughout India and deliver dependable spring solutions for defence, electronics, medical, pharma, oil & gas, irrigation, home appliances, and more.
